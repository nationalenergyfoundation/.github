# National Energy Foundation

### Empowering the Next Generation with Energy Education

**Our mission is to promote and cultivate an energy literate society.**

[Website](https://nef1.org/) · [About NEF](https://nef1.org/about-nef/) · [Programs](https://nef1.org/programs/) · [Contact](https://nef1.org/contact/)

</div>

---

For nearly 50 years, the National Energy Foundation (NEF) has partnered with utilities, educators, and communities to bring hands-on energy education to students and their families. From the classroom to the kitchen table, our programs help people understand where energy comes from, how to use it wisely, and how to stay safe around it.

## Our programs

| Program | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 💡 **[Think! Energy](https://nef1.org/programs/)** | Energy efficiency education with a proven school-to-home approach that reaches students *and* their families. |
| 🦺 **[Energy Safe Kids](https://nef1.org/programs/)** | Natural gas and electric safety resources that help organizations build an effective safety strategy. |
| 🚗 **[rEV](https://nef1.org/programs/)** | An electrifying, interactive presentation that gets students excited about electric vehicles. |
| 🍎 **[Teacher Support](https://nef1.org/programs/)** | Resources and support for educators bringing energy into their classrooms. |
| 📊 **[National Survey](https://nef1.org/survey/)** | A national survey measuring the energy literacy of high school seniors. |
